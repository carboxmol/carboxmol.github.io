About Carbox

                    
A systematic high-throughput survey and microenvironment workbench for anomalous carboxyl–carboxyl interactions and pKa shifts in high-resolution protein crystal structures.

What is Carbox?

                            
Carbox is an automated pipeline that screens tens of thousands of high-resolution (≤ 2.0 Å) X-ray protein crystal structures from the Protein Data Bank (PDB) to identify anomalous carboxyl–carboxyl pairs (Asp–Asp, Asp–Glu, Glu–Glu) with short O···O distances indicative of strong pKa perturbation and possible Low-Barrier Hydrogen Bonds (LBHBs).

                        

                    

                    

                        

                        

                            
CarboxMol Integration

                            
CarboxMol is the crystallographic computation engine powering this workbench. It provides real-space refinement, stereochemical validation, protonation state modeling, CXL macro scripting, and interactive 3D visualization directly from your browser via the local MCP server.

                        

                    

                    

                        

                        

                            
Methodology & Filters

                            
Three-criterion filter pipeline: (1) O···O distance ≤ 3.20 Å for carboxyl–carboxyl proximity, (2) Microenvironment burial assessment via nearest-water distance (> 3.0 Å = buried, ε ≈ 2–4), (3) Distance tier classification into Ultra-Short LBHB (≤ 2.55 Å) or Short H-Bond (2.55–3.20 Å) categories.

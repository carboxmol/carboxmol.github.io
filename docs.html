Crystallography Calculation Engine

Carbox

High-throughput macromolecular coordinate and geometry calculation library for detecting anomalous carboxyl protonation states, low-barrier hydrogen bonds, and microenvironment dielectric shielding.

  • Carboxyl spatial proximity screening ($d_{O\cdots O} \le 3.20\,\text{Å}$) and LBHB detection.
  • Microenvironment dielectric shielding ($\varepsilon_{\text{eff}} \approx 2-4$) & water burial index ($d_w$).
  • Quantum-mechanical 1D potential energy surface (PES) proton shuttle simulation.
  • Automated REFMAC5, Servalcat, and Phenix external restraint dictionaries (`exte dist`).

3D Workbench & Simulation Suite

CarboxMol

Universal 3D macromolecular crystallography workbench and molecular dynamics studio featuring real-time WebGL2 visualization, electron density contouring, and CXL automation.

  • Interactive WebGL2 3D viewer with Mode 0 binary CCP4 map streaming and decompression.
  • Real-time dual-mesh $2mF_o - DF_c$ and $mF_o - DF_c$ electron density carving.
  • Precision trackpad gesture engine for continuous density $\sigma$ level adjustments.
  • Carbox eXtensible Language (`.cxl`) macro scripting & GROMACS MD automation.

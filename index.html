CarboxMol & Carbox — Scientific Architecture, 6-Stage Validation & GROMACS Suite

1. CarboxMol 3D Macromolecular Crystallography Workbench & GROMACS Studio

CarboxMol (carboxmol.github.io) is an enterprise-grade 3D macromolecular crystallographic visualization workbench, electron density carving engine, and GROMACS molecular dynamics studio engineered by Suleiman M Hajizadeh (Institute of Molecular Biology, Ministry of Science and Education of the Republic of Azerbaijan; Western Caspian University). Working alongside the Carbox mathematical validation engine (project-carbox.github.io), CarboxMol enables structural biologists and crystallographers to inspect anomalous carboxyl–carboxyl interactions ($\text{Asp}/\text{Glu}$ dyads), shared-proton Low-Barrier Hydrogen Bonds (LBHBs, $d_{\mathrm{O}\cdots\mathrm{O}} \le 2.55\,\text{Å}$), and microenvironment dielectric shielding across 7,230 atomic-resolution ($\le 1.20\,\text{Å}$) Protein Data Bank (wwPDB) structures.

2. Six-Stage Crystallographic Validation Protocol (Murshudov Principles)

  • Stage 1 — Global Resolution & Data Quality: High-resolution diffraction limit ($d_{\min} \le 1.20\,\text{Å}$) and $R_{\text{free}} \le 0.22$ screening via Gemmi C++/Python coordinate parsing.
  • Stage 2 — 3D KD-Tree Carboxyl Contact Mining: Exhaustive symmetry-aware identification of intra- and intermolecular $\text{Asp}/\text{Glu}$ oxygen pairs within $2.20\,\text{Å} \le d_{\mathrm{O}\cdots\mathrm{O}} \le 3.20\,\text{Å}$ and steric clash rejection ($d < 2.20\,\text{Å}$).
  • Stage 3 — Thermal $B$-Factor Outlier & Vibrational Coupling Audit: Non-parametric Tukey IQR fence ($Q_3 + 1.5\times\text{IQR}$) and parametric $Z_B = (B_i - \mu_B)/\sigma_B \le 3.0$ filtering to eliminate disordered surface loop artifacts.
  • Stage 4 — Direct Electron Density ($2mF_o - DF_c$ & $mF_o - DF_c$) Real-Space Correlation: Singularity-protected Fisher $Z$-transformation ($z = \text{arctanh}(\text{RSCC})$) requiring $\text{RSCC} \ge 0.80$ and $Z_{\text{RSCC}} \ge -1.5$ to confirm experimental electron density support.
  • Stage 5 — Divalent & Transition Metal Chelation Veto: Automated exclusion of carboxylate anions coordinating $\text{Mg}^{2+}, \text{Ca}^{2+}, \text{Zn}^{2+}, \text{Mn}^{2+}, \text{Fe}^{2+/3+}$ within $d_{\mathrm{O}\cdots\mathrm{M}} \le 2.80\,\text{Å}$.
  • Stage 6 — Empirical $\text{p}K_a$ Shift & REFMAC5/Servalcat Restraints: Born desolvation and Coulombic microenvironment integration requiring $\max(\text{p}K_a) \ge 6.0$, with automated export of harmonic distance and planarity restraints for REFMAC5 and Servalcat.

3. Golden Standard PDB Benchmarks & Command-Line Interface (CLI)

Validated against flagship sub-Ångström crystal structures including 1OEW (Endothiapepsin at $0.90\,\text{Å}$), 1KZK (HIV-1 Protease catalytic dyad at $1.00\,\text{Å}$), 2DSX (Rubredoxin at $0.65\,\text{Å}$), and 1FKN (Human BACE-1). Researchers can launch the interactive 3D workbench via carboxmol 4LGN or run full crystallographic validation via carbox analyze 1OEW.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) · Tez-Tez Verilən Suallar

What is CarboxMol and how does it connect to Project Carbox?

CarboxMol (carboxmol.github.io) is the 3D WebGL macromolecular crystallography studio, real-time $2mF_o - DF_c$ electron density viewer, and GROMACS molecular dynamics workbench. Project Carbox (project-carbox.github.io) is the companion high-throughput crystallographic survey and mathematical validation framework for anomalous carboxyl protonation and Low-Barrier Hydrogen Bonds (LBHBs).

Where is the official CarboxMol & Carbox documentation located?

Full trilingual (English, Azerbaijani, Hebrew RTL) documentation is available on Read the Docs at carboxmol.readthedocs.io/en/latest and project-carbox.readthedocs.io/en/latest, on the interactive web portal at carboxmol.github.io/docs.html, and in the downloadable monograph CARBOX_MASTER_MANUAL.pdf.
